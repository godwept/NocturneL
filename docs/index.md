# Your music. Your device.

NocturneL is an **offline, terminal-themed Android music player** built for the music you already own. Select one local folder and browse, search, queue, and play without accounts or a connection.

<section class="site-section" id="features" aria-labelledby="features-title">
  <header class="section-heading">
    <p class="eyebrow">Designed for owned music</p>
    <h2 id="features-title">A focused player, not another service</h2>
    <p>NocturneL turns a selected folder into a fast local library while keeping control, files, and listening data on your device.</p>
  </header>
  <div class="feature-grid">
    <article class="feature-card terminal-panel">
      <h3>Your folder, deliberately scoped</h3>
      <p>Grant access through Android's system picker. NocturneL scans only the selected folder and its descendants.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="feature-card terminal-panel">
      <h3>Albums, artists, and search</h3>
      <p>Move between grid and cover-flow views, explore artists, search locally, and sort by artist, title, year, or play count.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="feature-card terminal-panel">
      <h3>Playback that keeps going</h3>
      <p>Use gapless transitions, shuffle, repeat, seeking, background playback, and Android lock-screen media controls.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="feature-card terminal-panel">
      <h3>An editable queue</h3>
      <p>Add tracks or albums, jump to an item, drag to reorder, remove and undo, or clear everything upcoming.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="feature-card terminal-panel">
      <h3>Terminal visualizers</h3>
      <p>Cycle between album art, a circular radar, and spectrum bands with adjustable sync and phosphor afterglow.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="feature-card terminal-panel">
      <h3>Portable playlists</h3>
      <p>Create and reorder playlists, import or export M3U/M3U8, and back up all playlists in one ZIP.</p>
    </article>
  </div>
</section>
