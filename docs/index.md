# Your music. Your device.

<p class="hero__lede">NocturneL is an <strong>offline, terminal-themed Android music player</strong> for the audio files you already own. Point it at a folder and play your library without an account, a subscription, ads, analytics, or an internet connection.</p>

<section class="site-section" id="features" aria-labelledby="features-title">
  <header class="section-heading">
    <p class="command-label">&gt; HELP /CAPABILITIES</p>
    <h2 id="features-title">Local playback without the service layer.</h2>
    <p>Select one music folder. NocturneL scans that selected folder and its descendants, builds a local library, and keeps playback and listening data on the device.</p>
  </header>

  <div class="feature-grid">
    <article class="feature-card terminal-panel">
      <span class="feature-code">LIB</span>
      <h3>Albums, artists, and search</h3>
      <p>Browse in grid, cover-flow, or artist views. Search locally and sort by artist, title, year, or play count.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="feature-card terminal-panel">
      <span class="feature-code">NOW</span>
      <h3>Playback that stays active</h3>
      <p>Gapless transitions, seeking, shuffle, repeat, background playback, media notification, and Android lock-screen controls.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="feature-card terminal-panel">
      <span class="feature-code">QUE</span>
      <h3>Editable queue</h3>
      <p>Add tracks or albums, jump to any item, drag to reorder, remove and undo, or clear what is coming next.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="feature-card terminal-panel">
      <span class="feature-code">VIS</span>
      <h3>Terminal visualizers</h3>
      <p>Switch between album artwork, circular radar, spectrum bars, and frequency-grid views with adjustable sync.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="feature-card terminal-panel">
      <span class="feature-code">PLY</span>
      <h3>Portable playlists</h3>
      <p>Create and reorder local playlists, import or export M3U/M3U8 files, and back up every playlist in a ZIP.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="feature-card terminal-panel">
      <span class="feature-code">LOG</span>
      <h3>Listening activity</h3>
      <p>Favorites, play counts, listening history, most-played sorting, and playback resume all remain local.</p>
    </article>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="site-section" aria-labelledby="requirements-title">
  <header class="section-heading">
    <p class="command-label">&gt; INFO /SYSTEM</p>
    <h2 id="requirements-title">Requirements and formats.</h2>
  </header>
  <div class="facts-grid">
    <div class="terminal-panel">
      <h3>Requirements</h3>
      <p><strong>Android 12+</strong> and a user-selected folder containing music files. Folder access is granted through Android's system picker. No network connection or account is required.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="terminal-panel">
      <h3>Candidate audio formats</h3>
      <ul class="format-list" aria-label="Supported audio file extensions">
        <li>MP3</li><li>M4A</li><li>AAC</li><li>OGG</li><li>Opus</li><li>WAV</li><li>FLAC</li>
      </ul>
      <p>Actual playback can vary with the device's Android media codecs.</p>
    </div>
