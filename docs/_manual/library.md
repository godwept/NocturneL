## Browse albums

Open **LIB** to see the local album collection. Select an album to view its artist, year, play count, and tracks.

Use **VIEW: GRID** or **VIEW: FLOW** to switch between the album grid and cover-flow presentation. In FLOW, swipe through covers and select the focused album to open it.

Use **SORT** to cycle through:

- **ARTIST** — artist, then album title
- **TITLE** — album title
- **YEAR** — release year
- **MOST PLAYED** — albums with higher qualified play counts first

The chosen view and sort mode persist between sessions.

## Use album actions

An album provides **PLAY**, **SHUFFLE**, and **ADD QUEUE** actions. Individual tracks can play immediately, join the upcoming queue, or be marked as favorites. The album favorite control and play count appear with its metadata.

Choose **SET COVER** to select a replacement image with Android's document picker. The selected cover takes priority over embedded and folder artwork.

## Browse artists and search

Open **ART** to browse artists and their album counts. Select an artist to see their albums.

Open **SEA** and enter text in **SEARCH LOCAL LIBRARY**. Matches are grouped under **ALBUMS**, **ARTISTS**, and **TRACKS**. Select an album or artist to open it; select a track to play it. Track results also provide favorite and add-to-queue actions.

## Rescan or change the source

In **SET**, choose **RESCAN LIBRARY** after adding, removing, or retagging files. Progress appears while NocturneL discovers and indexes files; **CANCEL** preserves the previous completed catalog.

If Android reports that folder access was lost, choose the folder again. **CHANGE MUSIC FOLDER** asks for confirmation when the source differs. Confirming clears favorites, listening history, play counts, and resume state because those identities belong to the old library. Cancelling leaves the current source unchanged.
